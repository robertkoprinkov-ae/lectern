    Finding the recording link

    For a Cassyni lecture you usually don’t need to: paste its address (Share › Copy, or the address bar) or its DOI, and Lectern looks up the recording. This works when the recording is public on Cassyni.

    For a recording hosted somewhere else, or if that fails, find the address of the recording itself. Your Mac makes this quick:

    1. On the Mac, open the lecture in Safari.
    2. Turn on Safari › Settings › Advanced › “Show features for web developers”.
    3. Choose Develop › Show Web Inspector, then open the Network tab.
    4. Start the video. Look for a request ending in .m3u8 or .mp4 (type “Media” or search for those).
    5. Right-click it › Copy Link. Paste it into “Recording link” here; Universal Clipboard carries it to your iPhone.

    If the recording can be downloaded, save it to iCloud Drive and use “Choose a saved file” instead. Files are kept on this iPhone, so they also play offline.

    For Cassyni lectures the board shows the slide being discussed. Swipe it left or right to look at other slides, and tap Reset to go back to the one being discussed. Tap the slide to open it full screen, where you can pinch or double-tap to zoom. The CC button shows captions when Cassyni has them.

    Playback keeps going with the screen locked, and the lock screen shows play, pause and skip controls. Your place in each lecture is saved on this device.
